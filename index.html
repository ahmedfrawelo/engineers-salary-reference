How the index stays reliable

Three rules keep the directory useful instead of becoming another stale list.

Official source first

Pricing pages, product documentation and provider consoles are the primary references.

Free is not trial

Time-limited credits, evaluation keys and conditional programs are classified separately.

Meaningful changes are tracked

Rate-limit reductions, card requirements, regional restrictions and discontinued offers are recorded.

Frequently asked questions

Does every listed provider stay free forever?

No. Plans and limits change. Every entry includes a verification date and official source.

Is a trial the same as a free tier?

No. Trials, promotional credits and conditional programs are shown as separate statuses.

Can I rely on the index for production decisions?

Use it for discovery and comparison, then confirm current limits in the official source before production use.
